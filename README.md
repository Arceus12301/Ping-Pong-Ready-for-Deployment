# Ping-Pong-Ready-for-Deployment
Ping Pong
Classic Pong reimagined for the modern era. Challenge your friends in Couch Co-op mode on a single device, or test your skills against AI opponents in Online mode.

Customize your player names, control your paddle with keyboard or touch drag, and compete for the highest score. Lightning-fast ball physics, smooth animations, and a sleek modern design make this the ultimate retro gaming experience.

Features:

🛋️ Couch Co-op: Local 2-player gameplay
🌐 Online Mode: Play vs AI (ready for real multiplayer)
📱 Mobile-First: Full touch support with drag controls
👤 Custom Usernames: Personalize your gaming experience
⚙️ Responsive Design: Works on any device
